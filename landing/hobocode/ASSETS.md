# Asset provenance (2026-10-16)

- `intro.mp4`, `intro-start.jpg`, `intro-end.jpg`, `og.jpg`: from aroido/hobocode main `App/Resources/Onboarding/onboarding-intro.mp4` (6.2 s, 720×1280 HEVC; original asset from the onboarding story mock, see that repo's `docs/video/README.md`). Re-encoded to H.264 (CRF 30, no audio, faststart) for all browsers. `intro-start.jpg` is frame 0, `intro-end.jpg` the 5.9 s stop frame used by the app, `og.jpg` a 1200×630 crop of the stop frame. The ⊗ position (cx 0.408, top 0.27, bottom 0.472) matches the app/prototype constants.
- `screen-home.webp`, `screen-map.webp`, `screen-alert.webp`: actual iOS captures from the App Store image work (aroido/hobocode main `18caaac` Debug build, dedicated iPhone 16 Pro Max simulator, 2026-10-14; raw `01.png`, `04.png`, `05.png`). Places, notes and tags are synthetic sample data; the latte photo attached to a note is a generated sample photo. NAVER map tiles and logo as rendered by the SDK. Resized to 660 px wide WebP (quality 80); no UI was edited or composited.
- `notification.webp`: actual iOS Lock Screen notification from the same work (raw `dining-arrival.png`, region x60 y2147 1201×284, the same region used in the App Store image). Synthetic note “둘이 오면 전골 소짜 + 볶음밥 하나…”. WebP quality 86. Korean on all locale pages and labelled as an actual development screen with an example note.
- `sign-*.png`: approved rough chalk hobo signs from aroido/hobocode `docs/brand/assets/hobosigns-rough-chalk-16` (same art as `HobocodeBrand.xcassets/HoboSign*`), trimmed and resized to 96 px.
- `chalk-mark.png`, `app-icon.png`, `wordmark.png`: unchanged official brand assets (see git history for the previous provenance note).
- Generated by `generate.mjs`: `town.svg` (illustrated neighbourhood from the onboarding prototype v6, seed 0x70B0, not a real place), `wear.svg` (HoboWear-style worn lettering mask). The rail-town silhouette and paint patches are inline SVG from `art.mjs`.
- Fonts: Black Han Sans, Nanum Gothic Coding, Saira Stencil One, Rubik Wet Paint (dates only) from the app's `App/Resources/Fonts`, subset to WOFF2. SIL OFL licenses shipped in `hobocode/fonts/`.
- Removed in this revision: Three.js/R3F globe and New York OpenStreetMap scene (`nyc.geojson`), globe fallback image, earlier Dynamic Island capture, earlier map/detail captures.
- No AI-generated app UI, no fake App Store URL.
