We do not treat readiness as a visual-only question.

A page is closer to ready when these conditions hold at the same time:

- The page can explain itself in one scan.
- The next action is obvious.
- The structure still makes sense on mobile.
- The content can survive a skeptical product review.

That is why our site work tends to mix copy, layout, and implementation in the same loop. If those pieces are reviewed separately for too long, the page starts looking finished before it is actually clear.
