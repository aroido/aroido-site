Privacy policy

This policy explains the records Hobocode keeps on this iPhone, map searches and automatic Live Activity processing, and your choices.

These pages apply to Hobocode. Ramgnote remains a separate app; its existing records are not automatically moved into Hobocode.

Records on your device

Hobocode stores note text, categories, place names and addresses you enter, saved coordinates, creation and edit times, selected photo copies and storage identifiers in app-private storage on this iPhone. Drafts, recovery inputs and trash are kept in the same place. Drafts and recovery inputs help preserve unfinished writing. The app does not check your iCloud account or separate records by account. If iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) is on, these records may also be included in the backup according to your Apple settings. There is no separate Hobocode sign-up.

Selected photos

The system photo picker gives the app the photos you choose. Hobocode creates app-owned JPEG copies, applies orientation and resizes images; it does not copy original EXIF, GPS or IPTC metadata into those copies. The original in Apple Photos is not deleted when you remove an attachment. Up to six photos can be attached to a note. The current version does not provide video attachment or in-app camera capture.

Location and nearby reminders

With permission, coordinates, accuracy and observation time are used to attach a location or compare saved facilities on your device. Automatic reminders are optional and require Always location, Precise Location and notification permission. When enabled, location can be used while the app is not in the foreground. Per-place nearby/away/notification times and reminder settings are kept locally to prevent repeat alerts; notification groups expire after 24 hours and are cleaned up when the app can run. This does not detect individual indoor shops or floors. You can turn reminders off in the app and change permissions in iPhone Settings. Manual writing remains available without these permissions.

Maps and place searches

Hobocode uses NAVER Maps and place search, with Apple search and reverse-geocoding paths. NAVER search sends the query you enter through a Cloudflare Workers relay to NAVER. The search request body contains the query, not your note text or current device coordinates. Apple search may be used depending on configuration, errors or limits. When you save a selected result, its place name, address, coordinates and source information are saved with the record on this iPhone. Apple place references can include a place ID and your alias. Apple search receives the entered search query. When looking up an address from a location, the app sends those coordinates to Apple for reverse geocoding.

iCloud data from earlier test versions

Earlier test versions (TestFlight) had optional iCloud sync. If you turned it on, the current app no longer reads or deletes data already sent to iCloud; you can delete it yourself in iPhone Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage). The first time you open the app after updating, it continues with the most recently used record space that was on this iPhone. If an earlier version used both the device record space and per-iCloud-account record spaces on this iPhone, the other records no longer appear in the app but stay on this iPhone, and they are deleted along with the app if you delete it. The app does not delete files while moving records.

Siri and Lock Screen visibility

Siri and App Shortcuts pass the requested note text to the app, which opens in the foreground and requires device unlock. Hobocode does not save a separate voice recording; Apple handles Siri under its own settings and policies. Lock Screen and Dynamic Island displays can expose note excerpts or place information to nearby people, depending on your visibility choices. For automatic Live Activities, the Cloudflare relay processes installation and authentication identifiers and APNs tokens. Apple APNs receives the delivery token and a display payload containing session/place/note identifiers and update and expiry information. Display requests do not include note bodies, photos or precise coordinates; display content comes from records on the device. Permissions, connectivity and iOS limits can prevent display. Ending an activity does not delete the original note.

Retention and deletion

Saved notes remain until you delete them. Trash can be restored within 30 days or permanently deleted earlier. Explicit drafts expire 30 days after creation; expired items and unreferenced photo copies are removed when the app can process storage. Recovery inputs can remain until they are saved, discarded or their related record is removed. Deleting the app can remove the records on this iPhone. Data an earlier test version already sent to iCloud, and iOS system backups, are stored separately from the app (in iCloud or on your computer), so deleting the app does not erase them. Manage them in your device settings or on the computer where the backup is stored; support cannot promise immediate deletion of every Apple-managed backup.

Protection and other services

Hobocode keeps records in app-private storage on this iPhone and uses iOS file protection. This is not a claim of end-to-end encryption or a separate app password. The app uses Apple Siri, APNs and map/search services, NAVER maps/search and Cloudflare relays. Advertising-enabled versions use Google AdMob; the advertising section below explains that processing. This website is hosted by Vercel, loads Google Fonts and includes Vercel Web Analytics for visits and some web interactions. Some pages, including the homepage, also load Google Analytics. Web requests can transmit IP addresses and browser information to service providers. App processing and website analytics are separate.

App advertising and privacy choices

Advertising-enabled versions use Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent management. Native ads appear among home memos and library place groups, and a banner may appear below a memo. No interstitial or Live Activity ads are included. Ads are requested as non-personalized ads; the app does not request ATT authorization to access the IDFA.

The app does not put memo text, photos, exact coordinates, addresses, search terms or an app account identifier into advertising requests. The advertising SDK may process IP addresses, device or app identifiers, crash and performance information, and ad impressions and interactions for advertising, measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention. An IP address can be used to estimate general location. Non-personalized requests do not mean that no data is processed.

Where consent is required, the app checks UMP consent information before requesting ads. When required, privacy choices can be changed through the advertising privacy options in app settings. Changing or withdrawing consent does not automatically delete information already processed by a provider. Google processing, retention and sharing are described in its privacy policy; the app does not promise a single processing country or immediate deletion of provider-held data.

Relay records and deletion requests

The automatic Live Activity relay stores authentication, session and delivery-control records, including encrypted APNs tokens, in Cloudflare D1. Expired authentication records are eligible for cleanup only when the required checks pass; records needed for active sessions or recovery can remain. Turning the feature off, ending a display or deleting the app does not establish that every server record or provider backup has been erased. Contact admin@aroido.com for questions or deletion requests. This policy does not promise a fixed server-wide deletion deadline or a single processing country.
