Account

You must be 14 or older to sign up, and an account found to belong to someone under 14 is deleted. You sign in with an Apple, Google, Kakao or NAVER account; accounts from different providers are separate Hobocode accounts and are not merged. After the first sign-in you choose a nickname of 2 to 12 characters that nobody else uses and that contains no banned words; you can change it once every 30 days. You are responsible for the accounts you sign in with. You can delete your account at any time in Settings > My profile; the account is deleted at once.

Your memos

Memos you make public or send by link remain yours. You allow the operator, free of charge and non-exclusively, to store, transmit and show them in Hobocode and its share links to the extent needed to run these features. This permission ends when you turn the memo's public setting off, turn the link off or delete your account, except that copies other users scrapped before then stay on their devices and server backups keep copies for up to 30 days. Post only content you have the right to post.

When another user scraps your public memo, a copy is saved as a personal memo on that user's device, with a line naming you as the source. That copy stays on their device even after you delete the original or your account, and the operator cannot delete it.

Free service, ads and changes

Hobocode is free and shows ads. When you open someone else's public memo in full for the first time, a full-screen ad may appear first. The operator may change or end features; important changes are announced in advance on this page or in the app.

Moderation

Content that breaks the community rules below may be hidden or deleted without prior notice. For repeated or serious violations the operator may delete the account. To object to an action, email admin@aroido.com.

Community rules

Public memos are notes that help other people who go to the same place. Hobocode does not tolerate objectionable or abusive content. Do not post:

  • Other people's personal information or private life: home addresses, phone numbers, licence plates, photos in which a person can be recognised, and the like.
  • Insults, hate, discrimination, harassment or threats.
  • Sexual content, or content that encourages violence or self-harm.
  • Illegal activity, illegal goods or scams.
  • Ads, repeated posts or content unrelated to the place.
  • Others' copyrighted work or trademarks without permission, or impersonating someone.
  • False statements that damage the reputation of a shop or a person.

Nicknames and public memos are checked against a banned-word list before they are posted. On any public memo you can choose Report (spam or ads, abuse or hate, personal information, unrelated to the place, other) or Block (that person's public memos and marks are no longer shown to you). A memo reported by 3 different people is hidden automatically, and the operator reviews reports and removes violating content, and deletes the account of a person who breaks the rules repeatedly or seriously. You can also report to admin@aroido.com.
